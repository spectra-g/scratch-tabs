# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas in good faith.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Disruptive trolling or bad-faith engagement.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, issues, pull requests, and community discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy. They may remove, edit, or reject content that violates these standards.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately via repository maintainers.
